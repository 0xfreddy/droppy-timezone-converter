# Changelog

## [1.1.0] - 2026-10-28

- Dot-matrix theme: a header toggle switches the display surfaces — title,
  city labels, times, deltas, day badges, the date, and the ruler numbers —
  to a glowing flip-disc panel face, with each city's time in its part-of-day
  color. The theme persists. Interaction surfaces (query field, zone editor,
  buttons) stay in system type.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-28

- First release: one expanded notch surface that converts a moment across
  your zones. Results first, controls below: scrubbing hour and
  15-minute-block rulers (drag, trackpad scroll, or tap) with a fixed
  centre needle, a day stepper, natural-language queries (“3pm New York”,
  “tomorrow 9:30 Berlin”, “mon 10 utc+5”), a persistent zone list edited
  in place, and per-row offset deltas. The hour ruler and each city's time
  carry the day's colors — blue night, orange morning, golden midday,
  purple evening.
